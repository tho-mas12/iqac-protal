Common Instructions 
1. Submit the Attribute wise Data Templates (in excel) and the corresponding evidence only as soft copies.  
2. One entry–one evidence principle – Every entry in the data template should be traceable to at least one 
supporting document. 
3. Submit the evidence in Scanned, Clear & Legible and Colour PDF documents (only in Portrait form).   
4. The rows marked with red colour in the data templates are SAMPLE records for your reference. Delete those 
records while entering your departmental data. 
5. Font for entering data in the templates: Cambria with 12 pts 
6. On submitting the Event reports, provide one consolidated report comprising: 
▪ Invitation/Brochure 
▪ Report of the event signed by HoD/Coordinator (First Page in the Department Letter Head) 
▪ The Report should spell out the Objectives and Outcomes of the Programme 
▪ Participants Attendance; for online activities, screenshots of the sessions and participants name 
list must be attached. 
▪ Geo-tagged Photographs in colour (with caption) 
▪ Feedback  
▪ Participant Certificates (in colour), if any.  
7. On submitting the Meeting Minutes, provide:  
• Agenda 
• Proceedings and Discussions 
• Photographs (Geo Tagged in colour with caption) 
• Attendance 
8. Scrupulously follow the below points while entering data in the templates: 
a. Academic-year relevance – Only activities/evidence falling within the specified academic year should 
be reported unless the metric specifically asks for a previous-year or multi-year data. 
b. Enter name of the Faculty with salutations such as Dr, Mr and Ms (Don’t use Mrs. / Prof. or the short 
name of the Faculty members)   Eg. Dr. D. Ravindran

d. Student Name should be entered as: Swathi J (Name followed by Space followed by Initial) 
e. Kindly follow the following format for entering Dates: 08.12.2025. For the total number of days, type the 
number as 1 or 5 (Not to be typed as One day or five days) 
f. Dates From-To should be mentioned as 22.07.2025 - 29.07.2025 (Avoid typing 22 to 29.07.2022)

h. For the Amount column, kindly mention only the amount in numerics (e.g., 10000), without using ₹ / 
Rs., commas, or other symbols 
i. Don’t merge rows. Enter each data separately. 
j. Data entered in one metric (category) must not be repeated in another metric. 
k. Data of faculty handling 2 departments must be entered only in their parent department. 
l. Only one entry must be made for each programme (Don’t make multiple entries for various resource 
persons).
